About Me

I design for the moment before the purchase.

Most ecommerce UX fails at the same point — the moment a customer hesitates. Saree shopping is emotional, tactile, and deeply personal. You can't touch the fabric, so the interface has to do the feeling for you.

My work sits at the intersection of craft and conversion: high-resolution detail views, honest fabric photography, weave-level storytelling, and checkout flows that never make a customer second-guess.

Across four saree ecommerce platforms at GrowthVia Digital, I've built design systems from scratch, run usability sessions with first-time online buyers, and shipped interfaces that moved real business metrics — not just portfolio pixels.
