About — the why behind the work.

I'm a UI/UX designer at GrowthVia Digital. For the last 3+ years I've shipped ecommerce experiences for saree and textile brands across India — building the kind of interfaces that make people feel confident before they click "buy."

The Story

I design for the moment before the purchase.

Most ecommerce UX fails at the same point — the moment a customer hesitates. That hesitation is the entire problem I work on.

G

Saree shopping is emotional, tactile, and deeply personal. You can't touch the fabric, you can't drape it in front of a mirror, you can't feel the weight of the zari. So the interface has to do the feeling for you.

That's the constraint I design against. Every product page, every zoom interaction, every weave-level story — it exists to close the gap between a digital screen and a physical product you'll wear to someone's wedding.

“A saree isn't a transaction. It's a decision someone makes for the most photographed day of their life.”

My work sits at the intersection of craft and conversion. On one side: high-resolution detail views, honest fabric photography, motif-level storytelling, weaver profiles. On the other: filters that actually filter, checkout flows that don't make you second-guess, and trust signals that mean something.

Across four saree ecommerce platforms at GrowthVia Digital, I've built design systems from scratch, run usability sessions with first-time online buyers, and shipped interfaces that moved real business metrics — not just portfolio pixels.

Before ecommerce, I spent time studying how people make slow decisions — the kind that take weeks, involve family opinions, and end with a purchase that has to be right the first time. Saree shopping is exactly that kind of decision. So is good UI.

How I Work

A process that ships.

Every project follows the same four-phase rhythm — flexible in pace, consistent in rigor.

01

Understand

Stakeholder conversations, user interviews, competitor teardowns, and analytics review. What's actually broken — and why?

02

Structure

Information architecture, user flows, and low-fidelity wireframes. The skeleton before the skin.

03

Design

High-fidelity screens, a reusable component library, and interactive prototypes for testing and stakeholder review.

04

Ship

Developer handoff with documented states, QA review on staging, and post-launch iteration based on real user behavior.

Principles

What I believe about design.

Four ideas that shape every decision — from the first wireframe to the final pixel-perfect handoff.

01
Clarity beats cleverness
If a customer has to think about how to use the interface, the interface failed. Delight is a bonus — clarity is a requirement.
02
Respect the craft
Saree-making is centuries old. My job isn't to redesign it — it's to translate it into pixels without losing what makes it valuable.
03
Measure what matters
Pretty screens don't pay bills. Every project I ship has a business metric attached — add-to-cart, drop-off, repeat purchase — and a plan to move it.
04
Design for the edge
The customer on a mid-range Android in a small town matters more than the one on a MacBook. Mobile-first isn't a trend — it's the majority.
